Garba Pandal – Gujarati Garba & Navratri Music

Garba Pandal is a curated Gujarati Garba and Navratri music experience featuring traditional Garba, Raas, Ramzat and popular festive tracks. Explore songs associated with Aishwarya Majmudar, Jigardan Gadhavi, Atul Purohit, Osman Mir, Bhoomi Trivedi, Maulik Mehta and other Gujarati music artists.
